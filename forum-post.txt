WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
- Lists every ship, weapon and fighter in your install from the main menu, and lets you blacklist the ones you don't want to ever see again.



Do you have 244 mods installed? Do you have 3294 ships? Do you need the 15th recolour of the Onslaught?
No. You don't. Block it and never see it again.
Is there a weapon in your modlist that is so comically overpowered that finding it for sale ruins the run?
Block it from markets. It still spawns on ships, so nothing breaks and nothing crashes.



Your choices are stored per installation, not per save. Curate your modlist once and every future playthrough inherits it.
The code is fully open source. You don't have to credit me. Claude Code built this anyway.

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]What Blocking Actually Does[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Ships are gone entirely.[/b] Removed from every faction's blueprints, so they stop spawning in fleets, stop being stocked in markets, and are hidden from the codex.[/li]
[li][b]Weapons and fighters are gone from markets only.[/b] They stop being sold anywhere, but still spawn on ships that were designed with them. This is deliberate: fully removing a weapon breaks every variant that mounts it and crashes on load. This way nothing breaks.[/li]
[li]Blocked entries are also hidden from the [b]codex[/b], all three categories.[/li]
[li]Your storage is never touched. Only shops get swept.[/li]
[/list]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Storage[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Saved to [b]saves/common/stopbloatingme/blacklist.json[/b], shared by every save you ever make.[/li]
[li]Nothing is ever written into a savegame, so adding or removing the mod can't corrupt one.[/li]
[li]Stored as plain ids. Disabling a mod leaves its blocked ids untouched, and they start applying again the moment you turn it back on.[/li]
[/list]

[spoiler=FAQ]
[b]Can I add or remove it mid save?[/b]
Yes, both. Nothing of this mod is ever written into your save. Removing it simply gives you everything back.
[b]Do I need to start a new game?[/b]
No. Blocks apply to any save the moment you load it.
[b]I blocked a ship but I still see it in a fleet.[/b]
Fleets that already existed in your save keep the ships they were generated with. Newly generated fleets respect the block. Give it a little time, or a little violence.
[b]Does this delete anything?[/b]
No. Nothing is deleted, no files are edited, no specs are unloaded. Blocked things are only prevented from being offered to you. Unblock and they come straight back.
[b]Why can't I block weapons from spawning on ships too?[/b]
Because that crashes the game on load. Every predefined ship loadout references its weapons by id, so removing one entirely leaves dangling references across your whole modlist. Blocking them from markets gets you the result you actually wanted without the crash.
[b]Why do blocked things still show in the codex until I restart?[/b]
The codex is built once when the game launches. Block something, and it's hidden from the codex the next time you start the game.
[b]Performance?[/b]
Negligible. The catalogue is indexed once at about 5ms. Enforcement is a handful of set operations per faction on load and once a day after that.
[b]Does it work with modded content?[/b]
That's the entire point. Every facet is generated from your installed mods, and source mod attribution comes straight from the spec, so it scales with however many mods you're running.
[b]What about (D) hulls?[/b]
They're deliberately not listed. Blocking a base hull already stops its (D) version, and blocking a (D) hull on its own could never do anything, so listing them would just be a button that doesn't work.
