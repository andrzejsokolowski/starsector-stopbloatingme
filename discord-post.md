# StopBloatingMe

**Lists every ship, weapon and fighter in your install from the main menu, and lets you blacklist the ones you don't want to ever see again.**

Do you have 244 mods installed? Do you have 3294 ships? Do you need the 15th recolour of the Onslaught? No. Block it and never see it again.

Is there a weapon in your modlist so overpowered that finding it for sale ruins the run? Block it from markets. It still spawns on ships, so nothing breaks and nothing crashes.

**What it does**
- Adds a **DE-BLOAT** button to the main menu. No campaign needed, no save to load.
- Three tabs: Ships, Weapons, Fighters. Click a row to block it, click again to unblock.
- **Hover preview** shows the actual sprite, so you can block by silhouette instead of trying to remember 3294 ship names.
- Live search plus multi-select filters with counts, built from your installed mods: size, designation, design type, source mod, mount, role and tier.
- **Bulk block/unblock** whatever the filter is showing. Filter to one mod's ships, one click, gone.

**What blocking actually does**
- **Ships are gone entirely** — removed from every faction's blueprints, so they stop spawning in fleets, stop being stocked in markets, and vanish from the codex.
- **Weapons and fighters are gone from markets only** — they still spawn on ships that were designed with them. That's deliberate: fully removing a weapon breaks every variant mounting it and crashes on load. This way nothing breaks.
- Your storage is never touched. Only shops get swept.

**Storage**
Saved per installation, not per save — curate once and every future playthrough inherits it. Nothing is written into a savegame, so it's safe to add or remove at any time, mid-save included.

Requires **LazyLib**. Open source, no credit needed. Claude Code built this anyway.
